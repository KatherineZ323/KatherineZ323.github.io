About Me

Design with
purpose

I'm a product and UI/UX designer who cares deeply about how technology shapes everyday human experiences — especially designing systems that are thoughtful, inclusive, and accessible to people with different abilities and backgrounds.

My path started with Informatics at the University of Washington, where I first explored human-centered design. That curiosity led me to Cornell University, where I'm pursuing a Master of Professional Studies in Information Science, focusing on product design, interaction design, and user research.

Outside design, I love traveling, hiking, skiing, discovering new food spots, and spending time with dogs. The small details in everyday life — moments that feel effortless or unexpectedly frustrating — often spark my best design ideas.
