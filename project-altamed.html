AltaMed Data & Analytics Portal

Designing a centralized dashboard discovery platform for internal healthcare teams.

Overview

This project was part of a collaboration between the Cornell MPS Information Science program and AltaMed Health Services — one of the largest Federally Qualified Health Center networks in the US. The goal was to design a centralized Data & Analytics portal that helps internal teams discover and access dashboards across the organization.

Acting as a UX/UI designer and researcher, I investigated the workflows and pain points employees face when trying to find and request access to dashboards. The result was a SharePoint-based platform that supports intuitive browsing by domain, robust search, and a streamlined access request flow.

The Problem

"We have hundreds of dashboards, but no one knows where they are or how to get access to them."

AltaMed's data and analytics resources were fragmented across multiple systems and teams. Employees spent significant time searching for the right dashboards, emailing colleagues for links, or simply not knowing what data assets existed. This created bottlenecks, inefficiency, and barriers to data-driven decision-making.
