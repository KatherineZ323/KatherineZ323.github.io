Overview

PawPals is an online pet fostering platform that connects pet owners, venue providers, and carers. It utilizes unused housing resources to offer professional pet care and pick-up/drop-off services, addressing issues like limited foster locations and unclear quality standards.

The platform earns revenue through service fees and an online shop, creating a safe, efficient, and community-driven pet fostering ecosystem. My role focused on designing the end-to-end user experience across the three user types: owners, carers, and venue providers.

The Problem

"Finding a trustworthy, available pet foster on short notice is stressful — and most existing solutions feel like Craigslist for animals."

Pet owners face a fragmented, trust-deficit market when looking for temporary care. Existing options (kennels, friends, informal apps) lack transparency around carer qualifications, real-time availability, and pickup logistics. Meanwhile, people with space and willingness to care for pets have no structured way to offer their services.

Research Process

1

User Interviews

Interviewed pet owners, current pet sitters, and people with available space to understand motivations, barriers, and trust signals across each user group.

2

Competitive Analysis

Analyzed platforms like Rover, PetBacker, and Pawshake to identify gaps in trust infrastructure, logistics support, and venue utilization.

3

Service Blueprint

Mapped the end-to-end service across all three user types to identify touchpoints, pain points, and opportunities for a smoother fostering experience.

4

Wireframing & Prototyping

Designed flows for booking, carer verification, real-time tracking, and the marketplace shop, iterating based on peer feedback and testing.

Design Solution

PawPals features three distinct app experiences — one for each user type — connected through a shared matching and logistics layer. Here are the key design decisions:

Feature 1 — Verified Carer Profiles

Each carer undergoes a structured verification process with background checks, pet care certifications, and space photos. Trust badges surface key signals to reduce owner anxiety during the booking decision.

Feature 2 — Smart Matching & Availability

Owners can search by proximity, pet type compatibility, available dates, and real-time carer status. The matching algorithm factors in pet personality and carer experience to reduce mismatches.

Feature 3 — Logistics & Pick-up/Drop-off

Integrated pick-up and drop-off scheduling with live map tracking reduces the logistics burden on owners and professionalizes the experience for carers. Add more detail about how this flow was designed.

Feature 4 — Online Shop

A curated marketplace for pet supplies allows carers and owners to purchase essentials, creating an additional revenue stream and keeping users within the ecosystem.
