Virtual Rural Healthcare Clinic

Designing an accessible AI-supported healthcare portal for elderly residents in rural Washington.

Overview

This 12-week capstone project focused on designing a virtual healthcare portal for elderly residents in rural Washington state — a population with limited access to in-person medical care and significant barriers to digital health tools.

Acting as both UX Researcher/Product Designer and Project Manager, I led a team through policy research, user research, concept design, prototyping, and stakeholder validation with UW Medicine. The final solution included a validated MVP and a dual go-to-market strategy.

The Problem

"Rural seniors face a double barrier: geographic isolation AND digital exclusion. Most telehealth platforms aren't built for them."

Rural Washington counties have physician shortages, limited transportation, and high proportions of elderly residents with chronic conditions. Existing telehealth solutions assume high digital literacy and reliable broadband — assumptions that don't hold for this population.

Design Solution

The portal was designed around three core principles: simplicity for low-digital-literacy users, AI-assisted triage to reduce clinical burden, and accessibility compliance for elderly users with vision/motor impairments.

Key Feature — Simplified Onboarding

Designed a guided onboarding flow with large text, audio support options, and a caregiver proxy mode for users who need assistance setting up their account.

Key Feature — AI-Assisted Symptom Check

A conversational AI triage tool helps patients describe symptoms in plain language, routing them to the appropriate care level and reducing unnecessary emergency visits.

Key Feature — Accessible Video Visit Interface

Redesigned the video consultation UI for large touch targets, high contrast, and simplified controls based on observed behaviors of elderly users.
