Olympians

A multiplayer cooperative party game set in Greek mythology — players unite as demigods to defeat powerful bosses through chaotic teamwork and strategic coordination.

Platform
iOS · TestFlight
Timeline
Jan – May 2026
Team
9 Students (Greek Frog Studios)
Course
INFO 5152: Advanced Game Design

Overview

Olympians is a multiplayer cooperative party game where players take on the role of demigods working together through their iOS devices to defeat powerful mythological bosses. The core gameplay combines fast-paced strategy, item management, and real-time communication — players must pass items, heal teammates, attack bosses, and coordinate under pressure.

Because each player has unique role advantages and limited resources, and because players cannot see each other's inventories, they must communicate out loud, share resources, and quickly adapt to changing boss attack patterns. Every boss introduces different challenges, making teamwork and coordination more important than individual performance.

Three Core Design Pillars

Pillar 1
Chaotic Teamwork
No single player can win alone. Every decision ripples across the team — coordinating under chaos is the game.
Pillar 2
Strategic Interdependence
Players hold different inventories and abilities. Success depends on reading teammates' needs and passing resources at the right moment.
Pillar 3
Mythology Immersion
From boss designs to UI iconography, every visual element deepens the feeling of inhabiting the world of the ancient Greek gods.

Studio & Team

Greek Frog Studios

A nine-person student studio formed for INFO 5152: Advanced Game Design at Cornell University. The team designed, developed, and shipped Olympians from concept to TestFlight over one semester.

My Role

As a member of Greek Frog Studios, I contributed mainly to the game's visual and design direction across four areas:

  • UI Design —Designed the in-game interface so players could quickly read game state during chaotic, real-time gameplay. Balanced visual style with usability in a high-pressure context.
  • Character-Related Design —Contributed to the visual identity of the Greek god and demigod characters, ensuring each felt distinct and communicated their role through visual cues.
  • Animation —Worked on animations that communicated gameplay actions clearly — boss attacks, player abilities, and item interactions — so players could respond instinctively without reading text.
  • Marketing Materials —Created visual assets for the showcase and distribution, including the app icon and promotional materials that captured the game's mythology aesthetic.

Design Process

1

Concept Development

The team developed the cooperative party game concept around Greek mythology, defining the three core pillars — chaotic teamwork, strategic interdependence, and mythology immersion — as guiding principles for every design decision.

2

Gameplay Specification

Designed the core loop: players receive unique roles and limited resources, bosses attack in patterns, and teams must communicate and pass items in real time. Each boss introduces escalating coordination challenges.

3

Art & UI Creation

Built the visual identity — character card designs, power-up iconography, in-game HUD, and animation — all grounded in ancient Greek aesthetics translated to a modern mobile game context.

4

Playtesting & Iteration

Ran multiple rounds of playtesting to identify friction points in both the interface and the cooperative mechanics. Iterated on UI clarity and boss difficulty curves based on player feedback.

5

Showcase & TestFlight Release

Prepared the final build for TestFlight distribution and a physical showcase. Created marketing materials and documentation to support the release experience.

Visual Design

The visual language draws from ancient Greek art — golden ratios, archway framing, and a color palette of deep bronzes, aged golds, and muted marbles — translated into a mobile game context that needs to communicate quickly and clearly during chaotic play.

App Icon

The icon combines the Omega symbol (Ω) with a sword — referencing both the game's name and the mythological world. The sky and cloud backdrop gives it a sense of scale and epic tone fitting for the App Store.

God Houses

The game features nine God Houses, each representing a distinct Greek deity with unique abilities, visual identity, and role in gameplay. Players align with a house at the start of each session, shaping their strategy and the items available to them.

Zeus · Aphrodite · Hecate · Demeter · Athena · Poseidon · Ares · Hephaestus · Hermes

Token Tiers

The token system is the core of the cooperative loop. Items come in three tiers — each more powerful and rarer than the last. Players must recognize tokens instantly during chaotic play, so visual clarity and tier differentiation were critical design constraints.

Reflection

01
Designing for Chaos
In fast-paced cooperative games, UI must communicate in under a second. This sharpened my understanding of visual hierarchy in ways that carry directly into product UX.
02
Animation as Communication
Animation is not decoration — it's the fastest channel for communicating state. Every frame of a boss attack animation needs to telegraph intent before the hit lands.
03
Playtesting Humbles You
Designs that looked clear on screen revealed confusion the moment real players touched them. Iteration loops in game design are tighter and more brutal than product cycles.
04
Theming as a Design System
Building a cohesive mythology aesthetic across icons, characters, UI, and marketing taught me how a strong visual system creates coherence across every touchpoint.

Game design thinking has deepened how I approach product UX — the same questions around feedback loops, user agency, and progressive disclosure apply whether you're designing a boss encounter or an onboarding flow.
